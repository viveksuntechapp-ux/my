Specialised dental departments

A specialist for every part of your smile

Most clinics have dentists who do a bit of everything. At Smile On, each area of dentistry has its own department, led by a dentist who focuses on that field. It's the difference between "we can try" and "this is what I do every day."

Department 01 · Everyday care

General Dentistry

This is the home base for the whole family. Regular check-ups with digital X-rays catch small problems before they become painful or expensive ones, and a professional clean removes the build-up that brushing can't.

  • Dental check-ups
  • Teeth cleaning · from AED 150
  • Fillings
  • Preventive care
  • Digital X-rays

Department 02 · Straighter teeth

Orthodontics

Straighten teeth and correct your bite with the system that suits your lifestyle, from clear Invisalign aligners to metal or tooth-coloured ceramic braces. For children aged about 9 to 14, early interceptive treatment can guide the jaw while it's still growing, which often makes later treatment simpler.

  • Invisalign
  • Metal & ceramic braces
  • Damon & self-ligating braces
  • Myobrace for children
  • Retainers

Department 03 · Save the tooth

Endodontics

Endodontics treats the inside of the tooth: the pulp and the tissue around the roots. If you have deep, throbbing pain or sensitivity that lingers, a root canal from a specialist can remove the infection, end the pain and keep your natural tooth. That includes complex cases and teeth that have been treated before.

  • Root canal treatment
  • Root canal retreatment
  • Apicoectomy
  • Post & core

Department 04 · Replace missing teeth

Implantology

Implants are the closest thing to growing a new tooth. Every case is planned on a 3D CBCT scan, so the implant goes exactly where the bone is strongest. Depending on your case, you may leave with a temporary tooth on the same day.

  • Same-day implants
  • Single & multiple implants
  • All-on-4 & All-on-6
  • 3D-guided implant surgery

Department 05 · Restore & rebuild

Prosthodontics

Prosthodontics restores teeth that are broken, worn or missing, so you can bite, chew and smile with confidence again. It covers everything from a single crown to a full mouth rehabilitation planned around your bite, comfort and appearance.

  • Crowns & bridges
  • Full mouth rehabilitation
  • Implant-supported teeth
  • Dentures

Department 06 · Healthy gums

Periodontics

Healthy teeth need healthy foundations. If your gums bleed when you brush, look swollen or seem to be pulling back, our periodontist can treat the cause and stop gum disease from loosening your teeth.

  • Gum disease treatment
  • Deep cleaning
  • Gum health maintenance

Questions

Choosing the right treatment

I don't know which department I need. Where do I start?

Book a general check-up. Your dentist will examine your teeth, take digital X-rays if they're needed and then bring in the right specialist.

Do I need a referral to see a specialist?

No. You can book directly with any department.

Do you accept insurance for specialist treatment?

We accept most major UAE insurers. Coverage depends on your plan, so we'll confirm it before treatment.
