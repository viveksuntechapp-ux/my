Same-day vs standard implants

Same strong implant, without the months of waiting

With a standard implant, you usually wait months with a gap or a removable temporary. Same-day treatment gives you a fixed temporary tooth on day one. When cases are correctly selected, long-term success rates are comparable to the standard approach.

Standard protocol

Implant now, tooth later

  • Implant placed, then left to heal
  • A gap or removable temporary for several months
  • Permanent crown after healing
Same-day at Smile On

Implant and tooth in one visit

  • Temporary crown made before your surgery
  • A fixed temporary tooth on the same day
  • Permanent crown after 3–4 months of healing
The process

Six steps from first scan to final smile

Each step has a clear purpose, and you'll know what's next before you leave the chair.

  1. Visit 1

    Consultation & 3D CBCT scan

    We assess your bone and plan the exact implant position in 3D. You get a complete written price before anything begins.

  2. Before surgery

    Your temporary crown is prepared

    We make your temporary tooth in advance, so it's ready to fit on the day.

  3. Implant day

    Implant placement

    The implant is placed under local anaesthetic. 3D-guided surgery is available for extra precision.

  4. Implant day

    Stability check & temporary crown

    We measure the implant's stability with an insertion torque device. If it meets the safe threshold, your temporary crown goes on the same day.

  5. 3–4 months

    Healing

    The implant fuses with your jawbone. Stick to soft foods for the first 6–8 weeks.

  6. Final visit

    Permanent crown

    Your custom permanent crown is fitted, matched to your natural teeth.

Is it right for me?

Who same-day implants suit

Same-day implants suit many people with one or more missing teeth. Same-day and All-on-4 protocols can also work with reduced bone, by angling implants into areas of denser bone. Your CBCT scan will show which option fits you.

Our promise: safety comes before speed

If your implant's stability reading doesn't meet the threshold for safe immediate loading, we won't attach the temporary crown that day. We'll explain this before surgery, and you'll leave with a removable temporary instead. Your implant then heals and is completed as normal.

Questions

Same-day implant FAQs

Will I really leave with a tooth on the same day?

In most suitable cases, yes. We measure your implant's stability during surgery. If it meets the safe threshold, your temporary crown is attached before you leave. If not, you'll get a removable temporary and the implant heals as normal. We'll tell you about this possibility before surgery.

How much does a dental implant cost in Dubai at Smile On?

A complete single implant (implant, abutment and crown) starts from AED 4,500, including 3D CBCT planning. Same-day implants cost a little more because of the extra planning. You'll receive one complete price after your assessment.

I've been told I don't have enough bone. Can I still get implants?

Possibly. Same-day and All-on-4 protocols are designed to work with reduced bone, by angling implants into denser areas. Your CBCT scan will show what's possible.

What can I eat after a same-day implant?

Soft foods for the first 6–8 weeks. Your temporary crown looks like a tooth, but the implant underneath is still healing.

When do I get my permanent crown?

After about 3–4 months, once the implant has fully integrated with your jawbone.
