Why Smile On

Why Satwa Residents Choose Smile On

Al Satwa has no shortage of dental clinics. The difference is who treats you once you're in the chair, and whether you have to be referred somewhere else for the next step.

Specialists in-house

An orthodontist, endodontist, oral surgeon, prosthodontist, periodontist and pediatric dentist all see patients here. No referral across town for the next step.

Same-day urgent care

Severe pain, a broken tooth or swelling? We keep slots open for urgent cases, so you can usually be seen the day you call.

Modern diagnosis, clear costs

Digital X-rays and modern equipment for an accurate diagnosis. Your dentist explains your options and what each one costs before anything starts.

Care for the whole family

From a child's first check-up with our specialist pediatric dentist to implants for grandparents, the whole family can come to one clinic.

FAQ

Questions About Our Al Satwa Clinic

What dental services does Smile On Dental Clinic offer?

We offer a full range of dental care under one roof: general and preventive dentistry, cosmetic treatments such as teeth whitening and veneers, orthodontics including Invisalign and braces, dental implants, root canal treatment, gum treatments and pediatric dentistry.

Where exactly is Smile On Dental Clinic in Al Satwa?

We're at Unit 213 on the 2nd floor of the AMKM Investment Building, 2nd December Street, Al Satwa, Dubai. 2nd December Street is also known by its older name, Al Diyafa Street.

What are your opening hours?

The clinic is open every day of the week, from 9am to 9pm.

Can I get a same-day appointment for a dental emergency?

Yes. We keep slots open for urgent problems such as severe pain, a broken tooth or swelling, so you can usually be seen the same day you call. Phone +971 55 896 1826 and tell the team it's urgent.

Do you treat children?

Yes. Our specialist pediatric dentist, Dr. Minu Mumtaz O.V., sees babies, children and teens, and we offer Myobrace for children with developing bite problems.

Do you accept dental insurance?

We work with a range of insurance providers. Send us your insurance details when you book and we'll confirm your cover before your appointment. Flexible payment options are available for treatment that isn't covered.

How do I book an appointment?

Call +971 55 896 1826, send us a WhatsApp message or book online. Our team will confirm your appointment time and send you a reminder before your visit.

What is the nearest Metro station?

The closest Red Line station is Max, previously called Al Jafiliya. From there it's a short taxi ride to the clinic on 2nd December Street.
