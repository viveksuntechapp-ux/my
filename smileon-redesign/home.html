Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Can't find yours? Call 04 347 2102 and our team will help.

What dental services do you offer?

Everything from check-ups and cleaning to Invisalign, braces, root canals, implants, whitening, gum treatment, wisdom tooth removal and children's dentistry. Each treatment is handled within one of our eight specialist departments.

Where is Smile On Dental Clinic?

Unit 213, AMKM Investment Building, 2nd December Street, Al Satwa, Dubai. We're close to Jumeirah, Al Bada'a and Karama.

What age should my child get braces?

Early interceptive orthodontic treatment usually happens between about 9 and 14, while the jaw is still growing. Dr. Manuel will advise whether your child needs treatment now or later.

How long does orthodontic treatment take?

It depends on the case and the appliance. After your assessment, your orthodontist will give you a personal timeline before you commit.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept most major UAE insurers. We'll confirm your coverage before treatment.

When are you open?

Wednesday to Monday, 9:00am to 9:00pm. We're closed on Tuesdays.
